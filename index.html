What we do

Systems that keep spaces comfortable and efficient.

From air conditioning to solar power, we design, install, and maintain solutions tailored to your building and budget.

  • Cooling systems

    Quiet, energy-efficient air conditioning for homes, shops, restaurants, and larger commercial spaces.

  • Heating systems

    Underfloor and space heating that delivers even comfort with low running and maintenance costs.

  • Solar thermal

    Harness the Cypriot sun for hot water and thermal energy with reliable, low-pollution systems.

  • Photovoltaic systems

    Clean solar power installations designed for lasting performance and minimal upkeep.

    Pool design and build with efficient temperature control and straightforward maintenance.

    Geothermal energy solutions and professional water treatment installation by trained specialists.
